\# 1. Purpose



This document defines every Artificial Intelligence capability inside TARDIS Den.



AI is not the product.



AI enhances the experience.



The room must remain fully functional even if AI services are unavailable.



AI should feel like a knowledgeable companion rather than a chatbot.



\---



\# 2. Design Philosophy



AI should never interrupt.



AI should never appear automatically.



AI only responds when the administrator intentionally asks for assistance.



AI should always remain contextual.



Example



Reading a book



↓



AI explains the topic.



Looking at Mars



↓



AI explains the image.



Writing a journal



↓



AI helps improve writing.



AI exists to deepen curiosity, not replace it.



\---



\# 3. AI Principles



AI-001



Context-aware.



AI should understand what object the administrator is interacting with.



\---



AI-002



Optional.



Every AI feature can be ignored.



\---



AI-003



Transparent.



AI-generated responses should be clearly identified.



\---



AI-004



Private.



Journal content is never used beyond generating the requested response.



\---



AI-005



Educational.



The primary purpose of AI is learning.

\---



\# 15. Privacy



Journal entries remain private.



Administrator data is never shared with other users.



Sensitive content should not be retained beyond what is required for the requested interaction.



\---



\# 16. Error Handling



If AI is unavailable



Show friendly message.



Examples



"AI is currently unavailable."



"You can continue exploring without AI assistance."



Never block the room because AI failed.



\---



\# 17. Performance



Target Response



2–5 seconds



Streaming responses preferred.



Large responses should render progressively.

\# 19. Non-Goals



AI should never



Automatically edit journal entries.



Automatically delete content.



Generate notifications without permission.



Replace external astronomy data.



Invent scientific facts.



Make decisions for the administrator.



\---



\# 20. Guiding Principle



AI should make TARDIS Den feel wiser, not noisier.



Every AI interaction should encourage curiosity, improve understanding, and preserve the calm atmosphere of the room.



The administrator should always feel in control.
